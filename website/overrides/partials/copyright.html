The HTML lives here, in website/overrides/, and is loaded through the `theme.custom_dir` setting in mkdocs.yml. No custom JavaScript is involved. Mirrors Material's partials/copyright.html and adds one line of build provenance published by website/macros.py: SITE_BUILD_DATE the deployed revision's date, `YYYY-MM-DD` SITE_REVISION the deployed revision, short form The date is rendered exactly as the deployed revision records it: no zone label, because at day granularity no zone is more correct than the offset the commit carries, and no conversion, because the footers of two builds of one revision are identical by sourcing the value in that revision rather than by normalizing a zone. Either value may be empty, for example when a local build has no checkout to read and no environment variable set; each is then omitted rather than rendered blank or guessed.
